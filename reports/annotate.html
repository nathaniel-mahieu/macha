What it does

Modulo candidates (mz.unity, generalised). For a rule with mass step Δ (¹³C, ¹⁵N, ³⁴S, ³⁷Cl, ⁸¹Br, ¹⁸O, ⁴¹K, Na–H, K–H, NH₃, H₂O, CO₂, …) and charge z, the charge-scaled masses M = z·m/z of related features differ by n·Δ, so they share the residue M mod Δ and n = round(ΔM/Δ) counts the steps; chains of any length (M, M+1, M+2, …) fall out of one search. Only canonical edges with gcd(n, z) = 1 are kept, so a z = 2 claim needs its own half-spaced peaks. Charge states and multimers, which are not constant mass differences, are matched by mapping both features to a neutral mass. Homologous series (CH₂, C₂H₄O) use the same residue test without co-elution. Co-eluting candidates are found by binary search of n·Δ/z targets in m/z-sorted RT blocks, which gives exactly the edges of a pairwise residue sweep at a fraction of the cost.

Bayesian edge evaluation. Every edge carries evidence: mass residual / its propagated σ, RT difference / apex uncertainty, Pearson correlation of the two raw traces over the union of both peak windows, the ¹³C intensity ratio against a Poisson carbon-count expectation, and (for consensus features) cross-sample intensity correlation. Under H₀ (coincidence) the evidence density f₀ is learned from decoy rules, the same search with Δ shifted to values no chemistry produces, with co-elution and shape modelled jointly because co-eluting coincidences are correlated more often. Under H₁ the densities are parametric. EM estimates a prior π per (rule, charge) and the H₁ parameters; each edge gets a posterior and a log-odds. Applying the fitted model to the decoys yields an independent FDR estimate, which checks calibration on real data where there is no truth.

Graph inference. Compound groups are the MAP partition under the edge log-odds (correlation clustering by greedy additive edge contraction), so a strongly negative edge can veto a merge that a single weak positive edge would cause in connected components. Within a group, isotope envelopes are chosen greedily by total log-odds (non-overlapping, per charge), other isotopes attach as fine-structure peaks, peptide envelopes are checked against an averagine pattern for a missing monoisotopic peak, and adducts / neutral mass come from the hypothesis explaining most monoisotopic features.

Accuracy against ground truth

An edge is true when both features come from the same compound. Baselines use the same candidates: "rule + RT" is the CAMERA-style deterministic rule, and adding a correlation cut is CAMERA with correlation grouping; groups are their connected components.

macha_recall_at_same_precision: recall of the posterior ranking when its threshold is set to match that row's precision. It is the like-for-like comparison: the posterior ranking dominates every rule-based baseline. On syn-hard (4-15 ppm co-eluting interferents, many coalesced) the default posterior cut of 0.5 is conservative, because coalesced features degrade mass, RT and shape evidence together.
