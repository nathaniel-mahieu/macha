What it does

The original macha seeded peaks with scale-space peak picking (Hamming smoothing at several widths, tracking maxima across scales) and then iteratively fitted and re-seeded skew-normal mixtures with numerical-gradient optimisers. Here detection is 0-dimensional persistent homology of the smoothed trace: a maximum is born at its height and dies at the saddle where it merges into a taller peak, so persistence equals topographic prominence, and all maxima are scored in O(n log n). Peaks need prominence ≥ 3σ and height ≥ 5σ above the baseline (σ = trace noise from the baseline module) and ≥ 4 observed centroids. Each feature then gets an apex RT (parabolic refinement), height, area, FWHM, SNR and an inverse-variance m/z with its standard error from the trace module's precision model, which the annotation and alignment modules use as uncertainty. Overlapping peaks can be refined by an EMG mixture fitted with an analytic Jacobian (fit_emg_mixture).

400 traces with 1-5 EMG peaks (SNR 3-300, tailing τ/σ 0.1-1) on zero, constant or drifting backgrounds, censored below 3σ. A detection matches a true apex within max(2 scans, half an FWHM).
